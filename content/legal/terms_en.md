## 2. What gitmgf is

gitmgf is a **directory** where people publish and find digital tools made for clinical practice and residency training, with a focus on primary care: apps, websites, calculators, spreadsheets, scripts, extensions, AI prompts and more.

gitmgf is **a repository of listings only**: it does not host, run or distribute the tools. It keeps a descriptive listing, screenshots and links to where the author makes each tool available (for example GitHub, the author's website or an app store). When you open a tool, you leave gitmgf.

## 10. Ranking and popularity

Results are ranked by relevance to the search, combined with a popularity score. The main parameters of that score are:

- clicks on tools from search results;
- clicks to open or download a tool;
- stars from signed-in users;
- the GitHub repository's stars, if there is one.

Older interactions count less: their weight halves every 30 days. Each visitor counts once per day per tool. The author's own clicks, the moderation and admin team's clicks and bot clicks don't count. You can also sort by "Newest", "Most starred" or "Recently updated". **Nobody pays for a better position.**
