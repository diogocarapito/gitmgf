# About gitmgf

gitmgf is a portal where clinicians — and anyone building tools for clinicians — publish, discover and reuse the digital tools they made: calculators, web and desktop apps, spreadsheets, scripts, browser extensions and AI prompts. The focus is **primary care (General Practice / Family Medicine)** and **residency**, without excluding hospital care.

## How it started

gitmgf grew out of the **"Inteligência Artificial e Literacia Digital"** (Artificial Intelligence and Digital Literacy) training, which we organised with the **Coordination of the Family Medicine Residency Programme of Lisboa e Vale do Tejo** (Coordenação do Internato Médico de Medicina Geral e Familiar de Lisboa e Vale do Tejo). It started in 2026 for **Family Medicine residents**, and also includes a **course for residency supervisors** (orientadores de formação).

During the sessions, residents and supervisors showed us tools they had built for everyday consultations and residency work — and asked where they could share them. gitmgf is the answer: one place, in Portuguese, to make them known and to find them.

## Why

Many professionals build useful tools — indicator templates, residency portfolio helpers, scores, export scripts — that end up scattered across messaging groups, personal repositories and links passed around by word of mouth. People who need them can't find them; people who made them get no visibility or credit.

## How it works

1. **Publish.** Anyone can create an account and submit a listing with a description, licence, data handling and links to where the tool is published.
2. **Review.** A moderator reviews every listing before it goes public. Review checks the [Submission Guidelines](/en/p/guidelines), not clinical correctness.
3. **Discover.** Search works in Portuguese and English, with or without accents. Ranking blends text relevance with real use: clicks from search, outbound clicks, stars and GitHub activity, with more weight on recent use.

gitmgf is **only a directory**: it does not host or run tools. There are no ads or paid placements, and pages use no analytics cookies or third-party services. Read more in the [Privacy Policy](/en/p/privacy).
