# Legal Disclaimer

**Version 2026-11 · Last updated: 16 November 2026**

## Tools published by third parties

The tools listed on gitmgf are created and published by their authors. gitmgf is only a **directory of listings**: it does not host, run or distribute the tools. When you open a tool, you leave gitmgf for an external website governed by the author's terms and privacy policy.

## No clinical validation

gitmgf **does not validate the clinical correctness** of tools, formulas, content or results. Moderation checks compliance with the [Submission Guidelines](/en/p/guidelines), not scientific accuracy.

Tools **do not replace clinical judgement** or the individual assessment of each patient. Always check results, doses and recommendations against reference sources and the local and national protocols and guidance in force.

## Declared certifications

Certification badges (for example CE, MDR, GDPR) show what the author **declared**, with the evidence they gave. gitmgf checks that this evidence exists, but does not certify, audit or guarantee the compliance of any tool. Some tools with a medical purpose may be medical devices under Regulation (EU) 2017/745; compliance is the manufacturer's responsibility.

## Profile information

Authors' profession and specialty are self-declared and not verified.

## Patient data

Do not enter patient data into tools whose data handling you don't know. Check the author's privacy policy and what the listing declares about data handling.

## Liability

gitmgf's liability limits are described in the [Terms of Use](/en/p/terms). If you find a problem with a tool, use the "Report a problem" link on its page.
