# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-11 · Last updated: 18 November 2026**

> This is an English translation for convenience. If it differs from the Portuguese version, the Portuguese version prevails.

gitmgf (<https://gitmgf.com>) is a portal where healthcare professionals and others share digital tools they built for clinical practice. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, on what legal basis, for how long, and what your rights are, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation — **GDPR**) and Portuguese Law No. 58/2019 of 8 August.

In short:

- We ask **only for the data needed** to have an account and publish tools.
- We use **no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies**, and no third-party scripts, fonts or services on our pages.
- Usage statistics are **pseudonymised** with a key that changes every day and is deleted.
- We **do not sell** data or use it for advertising.
- gitmgf **is not meant for patient data**: publishing data that identifies patients is forbidden.

## 2. Who this applies to

This policy covers people who visit the site, people who create an account (users, moderators and administrators), and anyone who sends us a report or a request, with or without an account.

It **does not cover** the tools listed on gitmgf that run outside it (apps, websites, spreadsheets, code repositories). Each one is its author's responsibility and has its own privacy rules. Read the data-handling information on each tool's page and its author's policies before using it.

**Data we do not want.** gitmgf does not process patients' health data. You must not include in text, screenshots or files any data that directly or indirectly identifies a patient (Terms of Use, section 6). If we find such data, we remove it immediately. The profession and specialty you state about yourself are professional data, not health data.

**gitmgf does not host tools.** gitmgf only stores listings and screenshots. When you open or download a tool, you leave gitmgf for the author's site, which handles your data under its own rules (see section 2). gitmgf only records, in pseudonymised form, that the link was opened (section 3, "Usage measurement").

## 4. Why we use data and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Create and manage your account, authenticate you and keep you signed in | Account, authentication | Performance of a contract — the Terms of Use (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Publish and display your tools with your name, profession, specialty and GitHub link | Account, published content | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Review submissions, handle reports, give reasons for moderation decisions and handle appeals | Content, interactions, moderation, communications | Legal obligation — Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, the Digital Services Act (Art. 6(1)(c)) — and legitimate interest in keeping the portal safe and reliable (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Deliver suggestions you send to a tool's author, by email and in their gitmgf notifications; share your email with the author only if you allow it | Account, interactions | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); consent to share your email (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you withdraw by not ticking the option again |
| Send emails needed for the service (sign-in, submission status, decisions, certification expiry notices) | Account, communications | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Calculate tool popularity and rank searches; show aggregate statistics to authors; understand which tools are missing (searches with no results) | Usage measurement (pseudonymised), stars | Legitimate interest in providing relevant search and improving the service (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Keep the service secure and prevent abuse and fraud (spam, score manipulation), limit excessive requests | Technical logs, usage measurement, account | Legitimate interest in the security of the service and its users (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Backups and disaster recovery | All database data and files | Legitimate interest in service continuity (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Respond to your rights requests and to requests from authorities; defend legal claims | The data needed for each request | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

**Legitimate interests.** Where we rely on legitimate interest, we have assessed that the processing is necessary and does not override your rights, because: we use pseudonymous identifiers that change daily and cannot follow a person across days; we build no individual profiles; we use no cookies for measurement; and raw data is deleted after 180 days. You can object to this processing (section 10).

**No automated decisions with significant effects.** We make no decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you (GDPR Art. 22). Two automatic mechanisms exist, always followed by human review:

- a tool that receives several reports of dangerous content or a security issue is **temporarily hidden** until a moderator reviews it;
- popularity ranking uses the signals described in the Terms of Use (section 10) and ignores automated (bot) clicks and the author's own clicks.

**We send no marketing.** If we ever want to send a newsletter, we will only do so with your prior, specific consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 5. What other people can see

When you publish a tool, the following become **public** on that tool's page: your display name, profession, specialty (if given), a link to your GitHub profile (if given), and all of the tool's content, including declared certifications. The total star count is public; **who** gave the stars is not.

When you send a suggestion, the tool's author sees your display name and the message; they also see your email **only if you allow it** on the form (they can then reply to you directly).

Your email, workplace, sessions, searches, reports and personal statistics are **not public**. Moderators and administrators can see them only as far as their role requires.

## 6. Cookies

We only use **strictly necessary cookies**, which are exempt from consent under Portuguese Law No. 41/2004 of 18 August, as amended. That's why we don't show a cookie consent banner.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `__Host-gitmgf_session` | Keep you signed in | Up to 30 days of inactivity, or until you sign out |
| `gitmgf_csrf` | Protect forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) | Browser session |
| `gitmgf_lang` | Remember your chosen language (PT/EN) | 1 year |
| `gitmgf-theme` (browser local storage, never sent to the server) | Remember the light or dark theme, if you choose one | Until you clear it in your browser |

We use no analytics, advertising or social media cookies, and no similar technologies (pixels, fingerprinting).

## 10. Your rights

Under GDPR Articles 15–22, you have the right to:

- **Access** — know what data we process and get a copy;
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data;
- **Restriction** — ask us to pause processing in certain situations;
- **Portability** — receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **Object** — object to processing based on legitimate interest (section 4), on grounds relating to your particular situation;
- **Withdraw consent** at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing already carried out.

**Self-service tools** (under *Settings*, when signed in):

- edit your profile (rectification);
- **Export my data** — a JSON file with your profile, linked accounts, tools, revisions, stars and reports you filed (access and portability);
- **Delete my account** (erasure).

For any other request, write to [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]]. We reply within **one month**, which can be extended by two further months for complex requests (GDPR Art. 12); if so, we'll tell you. Exercising your rights is free. We may ask for information to confirm your identity, for example by asking you to write from your account's email address.

**Right to complain.** You can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the **Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**, Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, Portugal, <https://www.cnpd.pt>, or with the authority of the EU Member State where you live or work. We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

## 11. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including: encrypted connections (HTTPS/HSTS); passwords stored only as salted scrypt hashes, with sign-in attempt limits; invite, confirmation, reset and session codes stored only as irreversible hashes, with attempt limits; protected cookies (`Secure`, `HttpOnly`, `SameSite`); CSRF protection and a strict content security policy; removal of metadata (such as GPS location) from uploaded images; restricted, logged access to moderation and administration functions; EU servers; backups encrypted in transit; and regular security updates.

If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the CNPD within 72 hours where required and inform affected people where there is a high risk (GDPR Articles 33 and 34).

## 12. Minimum age

gitmgf is for adults. You must be at least **18** to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

We may update this policy, for example when we change service providers or add features. The version and update date are shown at the top. For significant changes, we give at least **30 days'** notice on the site and, where possible, by email. Previous versions are available on request.
